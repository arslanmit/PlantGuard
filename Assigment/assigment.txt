# Module: Multi-Modal Chatbots

## Assignment Title: Developing an Early Plant Disease Detection Multi-Modal Chatbot — *PlantGuard*

### Overview

Timely and accurate identification of foliar diseases is critical to protect crops and reduce unnecessary chemical treatments. Small-scale farmers often rely on visual inspection, which can be delayed, inconsistent, or inaccurate. In this assignment, you will develop **PlantGuard**—a proof-of-concept multimodal chatbot that supports early detection of plant diseases through AI-powered analysis.

### Capabilities

* Process a photograph of a symptomatic leaf captured on a mobile device
* Capture a spoken description of observed symptoms
* Accept text-based follow-up questions from the user

---

## Learning Outcomes

* **LO1.** Innovate and design multimodal chatbots that process and respond to text, voice, and visual inputs, leveraging modern NLP and NLG to enhance conversational capability.
* **LO2.** Communicate comprehensive ethical considerations and regulatory compliance strategies for multimodal chatbots, addressing data privacy, bias mitigation, and responsible AI.
* **LO3.** Execute practical development and deployment of multimodal chatbots with user-centric design, data integration, and solutions to real-world constraints.

---

## Final Technology Choices

* **Platform & UI:** Streamlit (+ `streamlit-webrtc` for microphone) on Google Colab; GitHub integration
* **Vision:** ResNet50 (ImageNet-1K pretrained) fine-tuned on PlantVillage
* **Speech (Primary Disease Classifier):** MFCC features → CNN-LSTM (PyTorch)
* **ASR (for Transcripts & WER):** Whisper-tiny via Hugging Face Transformers (local)
* **Text QA:** BERT-based model (DistilBERT) fine-tuned on plant-care FAQ
* **Fusion:** Concatenate ResNet50 penultimate features (2048-d) with DistilBERT \[CLS] (768-d) → MLP head for final disease prediction
* **Data & I/O:** TensorFlow Datasets (PlantVillage), librosa, soundfile, Pillow, OpenCV
* **Training & Tracking:** PyTorch + **TensorBoard** (selected)
* **Security:** Local processing; temporary audio deletion; HTTPS tunnel for UI

## Tasks & Weighting

### 1) Environment Setup (10%)

* **Libraries:** PyTorch, TorchVision, Transformers, TensorFlow Datasets, OpenCV (headless), Pillow, librosa, soundfile, scikit-learn, **TensorBoard**, Streamlit, `streamlit-webrtc`.
* **API Keys:** Not required (local processing).
* **Colab + GitHub:** Clone, develop, commit/push (Method B).

### 2) Data Acquisition & Exploration (15%)

#### 2.1 Visual Data

* **Dataset:** PlantVillage (\~54k images, 38 classes) via TFDS (ImageFolder fallback).
* **Exploration:** Sample healthy vs. diseased leaves; plot class distributions; note imbalance.

#### 2.2 Voice/Text Data

* **Synthetic Voice:** Diverse, clear symptom descriptions (multiple speakers/noise levels).
* **FAQ Corpus:** Curated plant-care Q\&A pairs.
* **Exploration:** Transcribe a subset with Whisper-tiny; tokenize text; inspect vocabulary and coverage.

### 3) Preprocessing (15%)

#### 3.1 Image Pipeline

* Resize/normalize to 224×224 (ImageNet mean/std).
* Augment: random resized crop, horizontal flip, color jitter (optional rotations).

#### 3.2 Audio Pipeline

* Resample to **16 kHz mono**.
* Compute **MFCCs** (±Δ, ΔΔ) with fixed time frames (e.g., 5 s) and pad/truncate.

#### 3.3 Text Pipeline

* Lowercasing, light cleanup, stopword filtering where appropriate, tokenization.
* Prepare QA examples with aligned answer spans for fine-tuning.

### 4) Model Design (20%)

#### 4.1 Vision (ResNet50)

* Replace final FC for target classes; fine-tune on PlantVillage.
* Schedule: freeze backbone initially; unfreeze for joint training.

#### 4.2 Speech & Text

* **Speech classifier:** **MFCC → CNN-LSTM** to predict disease class from voice descriptions.
* **ASR (evaluation aid):** Whisper-tiny to obtain transcripts for **WER**; not used for classification.
* **Text QA:** Fine-tuned **DistilBERT** on plant-care FAQ.

#### 4.3 Multimodal Fusion

* Extract **image embedding** (ResNet50 penultimate, 2048-d) and **text embedding** (DistilBERT \[CLS], 768-d).
* Concatenate → **MLP head** → final disease prediction.

### 5) Training & Evaluation (20%)

#### 5.1 Training

* Separate loops for **vision**, **speech CNN-LSTM**, and **text QA**.
* Final **fusion** fine-tuning (encoders frozen or partially trainable).
* **TensorBoard:** log loss, accuracy, macro-F1, confusion matrices, and key hparams.

#### 5.2 Metrics

* **Vision:** Accuracy, per-class F1, confusion matrix.
* **Speech:**

  * CNN-LSTM: Accuracy & macro-F1 on labeled audio.
  * ASR: **WER** on validation set (Whisper-tiny).
* **Text QA:** Exact Match (EM) and F1 on held-out FAQ.
* **Fusion:** Overall diagnostic accuracy and macro-F1.

### 6) Deployment & User Testing (10%)

#### 6.1 Streamlit UI

* **Image Upload:** Leaf photos.
* **Voice Input:** Microphone via `streamlit-webrtc` and file upload option.
* **Text Input:** Follow-up questions.
* **Fusion Action:** When an image and a transcript exist, run fusion and show class probabilities.
* **Access:** HTTPS tunnel (e.g., Cloudflare Quick Tunnel); responsive layout; clear labels.

### 7) Ethical & Regulatory (10%)

* **Privacy (GDPR):** Local processing; **temporary audio deletion** post-inference; no PII storage.
* **Bias:** Document class imbalance; consider class weighting; report per-class metrics.
* **Responsible AI:** Show confidence scores; include a clear agronomic-advice disclaimer.
